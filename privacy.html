Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tune Time ("Tune Time", "we", "us", or "the App") handles information when you use the Tune Time application.

1. Information You Provide

Tune Time may allow you to create player profiles, including player names, colors, and character selections.

This information is used to provide the player profile, game, and scoring functionality.

2. Apple Music

Tune Time uses Apple's MusicKit framework to provide Apple Music-related functionality.

When you authorize access to Apple Music, Tune Time may access the information and functionality required to provide the App's music features.

Tune Time does not receive or store your Apple Music password.

Apple Music authentication, subscription information, and related services are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policies and terms.

3. iCloud

Tune Time may use Apple's iCloud services to synchronize supported application data across your devices.

Data stored or synchronized through iCloud is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. In-App Purchases

Tune Time may offer optional in-app purchases for additional playlist content.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. Tune Time does not receive or store your payment card information.

5. Third-Party Services

Tune Time may rely on services provided by Apple, including Apple Music, MusicKit, the App Store, and iCloud.

These services are operated by Apple and are governed by Apple's applicable terms and privacy policies.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Tune Time does not use personal data for targeted advertising or sell personal information to advertisers.

If analytics, crash reporting, advertising, attribution, or other third-party tracking technologies are introduced in a future version of Tune Time, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use personal information for targeted advertising.

8. Data Retention

Information stored locally by Tune Time remains on your device until it is removed through the App or by deleting the App, subject to the normal behavior of the operating system.

Information synchronized through iCloud may remain in your iCloud account according to Apple's services and retention mechanisms.

9. Your Choices

You can manage Apple Music permissions through your device settings.

You can manage iCloud functionality through your Apple Account and device settings.

You can remove Tune Time from your device at any time.

10. Children's Privacy

Tune Time is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be published on this page together with a revised "Last updated" date.
