Reporting a Problem

When contacting support, please include as much relevant information as possible.

  • Device model
  • iOS, iPadOS, or tvOS version
  • Tune Time version
  • Description of the problem
  • Steps to reproduce the problem

Screenshots can also be helpful when reporting visual or gameplay issues.

Apple Music

Tune Time uses Apple's MusicKit framework for its Apple Music-related functionality.

If music functionality is not working as expected, please check that your device has an internet connection and that Tune Time has the required Apple Music permissions.

In-App Purchases

Additional playlists may be available as optional in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. If a purchase is not available after completing the transaction, please restart Tune Time and use the available restore-purchases functionality.

If you continue to experience a problem with an App Store transaction, please contact Apple Support.

iCloud Synchronization

Tune Time may use iCloud to synchronize supported app data across your Apple devices.

Make sure that you are signed into the same Apple Account on your devices and that iCloud is enabled.
